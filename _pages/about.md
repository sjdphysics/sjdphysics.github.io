Hi! I am a postdoctoral researcher in condensed matter physics at McGill University. My research explores how periodic driving can engineer novel quantum states of matter. Using Floquet theory, I study how time-dependent fields can reshape electronic band structures and enhance interaction effects, opening new routes toward exotic phases such as flat-band and topological superconductivity. I am particularly interested in the fundamental challenges of driven quantum systems—including heating and the preparation of Floquet states—and in developing theoretical strategies to overcome them. More broadly, I am fascinated by how nonequilibrium dynamics can provide new ways to design and control quantum materials beyond what is possible in equilibrium.
