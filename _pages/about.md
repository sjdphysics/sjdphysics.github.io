Hi! I am a postdoctoral researcher in condensed matter physics at McGill University. My current research explores how periodic driving can be used to engineer novel quantum states of matter. Using Floquet theory, I study how time-dependent fields reshape electronic band structures that can modify the topological nature and also enhance interaction effects, opening new routes toward exotic phases such as flat bands and topological superconductivity. I am particularly interested in the fundamental challenges of driven-dissipative quantum systems—including heating and population of Floquet states—and in developing theoretical approaches to overcome them.

I am also interested in broader aspects of condensed matter physics, including correlation effects, disorder in topological systems, and other nonequilibrium phenomena. I am always open to new collaborations and opportunities to broaden my knowledge. Feel free to reach out!
